Amasser

An unofficial browser extension that collects KeyForge decks from your Master Vault account and syncs them to your Decks of KeyForge or The Crucible Online accounts.

Chrome Web Store Firefox Add-ons Source
  • After an initial sync, only newly scanned decks are synced — an initial sync of 1000 decks to DoK can take less than 5 minutes.
  • Syncs run in the background and are atomic: if an error occurs, the next sync picks up where it left off.
  • Optionally syncs daily while the browser is open and on each visit or deck scan in Master Vault.
  • Option to add Decks of KeyForge links to Master Vault.
